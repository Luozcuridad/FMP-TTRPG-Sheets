Inventory

All players are granted a Weapon & Sub Weapon listed below, as well as a communicator. Other useful items and weapons may be found on the battlefield or resting at the AMAZON bases.

Main Weapon

  • SWITCH Gun
  • Thunderbuss
  • Dual Side CharmsYellow Skirt Exclusive
  • Long WandYellow Skirt Exclusive
  • Scramble GownGreen Skirt Exclusive

Sub Weapon

  • Bluetape cutting knife
  • Hacking tool
  • Chip remover
  • Scouter (Binoculars)
  • Heater (Side pistol)

Bellnecks are allowed, but will be given a Bell charm hidden in their inventory. If the bell is discovered, the player it belongs to is outed to other players.

Pilots

Operators are limited to single handed weapons for use on the battlefield (barring skirt exclusive weapons) unless otherwise given by other players.

All Craft have durability, once downed a pilot must escape & fight on foot.

Recommended high Mechanical (Brains).

Strike BUNI/RABIT Pilot

  • Transportation for AMAZON forces.
  • Grants mobility to other troops to assist on battlefield.
  • Low grade attack power against ground & air forces.

Swan/Atmosphighter/HAARE Pilot

  • These craft can take out larger ground targets, or combat against faster flying FREMP drones/craft.
  • Grants support for ground troops when not engaged in ace combat.
  • High grade attack power against ground opponents.

BROOM Pilot Yellow Skirt Exclusive

  • Meant for high speed recon, and quick in-and-out attack maneuvers.
  • No weaponry on vehicle (Side Charms Recommended).
  • Can carry a maximum of 2 passengers (at high risk).

Armor

Scramble Gown Green Skirt Exclusive

  • Single person armor, High defense against physical attack.
  • Fixed Main weapon: Chainsaw sword.
  • Armor can be damaged to the point of being disabled, leaving User defenseless.

Stats

Stats determine what a Player character is proficient in. Each stat will be assigned 1 dice (D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, D20) and may only be used once. i.e. two stats cannot start with the same dice.

WeaponsFight

While every AMAZON fighter has some weapon's know-how, there can be quite a difference in the skills of a mercenary force. Weapons determine how proficient Characters are with a gun, blade, or other weapon/vehicles. Essential for fending off FREMP forces.

EvasionFlight

Useful for evading attacks both on the ground, and in the air. Evasion is essential when all it takes is one well placed RTD to change the tide of battle. These skills will determine how well your character will be able to escape capture or stealthily infiltrate a benefactor stronghold.

StrategyBrains

Strategy is a tech savvy engineer or leader's best friend. Strategy is useful for sussing out an ambush, remove chips from Maids, or bypassing FREMP security measures. A high strategy could be the difference between an easy win, or a total loss.

Raw StrengthBrawn

Is your fighter more physically equipped? Could they break open a Benefactor's head casing with their own hands? Raw Strength allows for your character to go toe to toe with robot foes without a weapon and win... maybe.

RallyCharm

Along with player characters there may be other AMAZON fighters and players on the battlefield or in the sky. Rallying these NPC characters to attack is essential for the survival of a squadron. High Rally may introduce support to a battle, or a distraction to pull FREMP forces away from the main party.

WillpowerGrit

How well can your character get hit and keep going? This stat is helpful keeping yourself moving after long battles, recovering from blue tape attacks, or staving off the effects of Cupid arrays.

Skills

Skills are the twenty checkboxes on your character sheet. Marking a skill means your character is trained in that field and can attempt things others cannot. Choose the ones that fit your character's story, and remember that your branch and stats will shape how you use them.

Leader

Natural commanders who keep a squad together when a battle turns ugly. Useful for coordinating other fighters.

Melee Fighter

Trained in blades, claws, and bare-handed brawling. The first choice for facing robots up close.

Heavy Gunner

Handles large, loud weapons that pin down FREMP forces and clear a path for the team.

Sniper

Patient and precise. Takes out targets from a distance before they know anyone is there.

Pilot

Skilled with AMAZON craft, from transports to fighters. Required to fly the vehicles listed under Pilots.

Engineer

Builds, repairs, and modifies gear and vehicles, and can keep a damaged craft flying a little longer.

Hacker

Breaks into FREMP systems, unlocks doors, and shuts down security. Also handy for removing chips from captured Maids.

Medic

Patches up wounds and helps allies recover from blue tape, Cupid arrays, and other nasty effects.

Rescuer

Specializes in pulling captured damsels and teammates out of trouble, even from a POW pole.

Escape Artist

Slips out of restraints, cages, and tight spots. A must for anyone who expects to be captured.

Analyst

Reads the battlefield, spots patterns in FREMP behavior, and notices the detail everyone else missed.

Spy

Blends in, listens in, and gathers secrets. Skilled at going unnoticed inside a benefactor stronghold.

Recon

Scouts ahead, finds safe routes, and warns the squad about ambushes.

Charming

Talks their way past guards, wins over civilians, and gets NPCs to lend a hand.

Well-Prepared

Always seems to have the right tool in a pocket. Once per mission, produce a useful item the GM approves.

Cold-Blooded

Keeps a steady hand and a clear head when everything goes wrong, and makes the hard calls without flinching.

Scavenger

Finds useful parts, ammo, and supplies in wrecks and ruins that others would walk past.

Wealthy

Comes from money or has funds to spare. Can pay for favors, bribes, and better gear.

Magical

Has real magical talent. Intended for Yellow Skirts, who must also track their extra magic tokens.

Lucky

Fortune has a habit of smiling on this character, right when it matters most.
