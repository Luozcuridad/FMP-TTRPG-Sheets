Faction

The French Maid Planet (FMP) is a creation of the late John Staton (We-r-nomad). His worldbuilding & art inspired countless creatives online over the years, and now I hope to keep his memory going by setting up TTRPG sessions & campaigns featuring his AMAZON army in their battle against the Robotic FREMP overlords. We miss you, friend.

Your Mission

You are a new recruit to the AMAZON mercenary forces & tasked with the extermination of the villainous robot benefactors that have made it a mission to secretly enslave and bind gorgeous damsels, putting them to work as kinky, latex adorned maids in their abodes.

The Benefactors have played nice with the universe, putting forth a mask that paints them as helpers among the galactic community, but in secret many have under the table dealings with authorities and governments to turn a blind eye to their dealings. As such, AMAZON forces are barely acknowledged among the wider populace, or in some cases villainized. But as a new recruit you have been witness to their true actions of abducting and mind controlling friends, family, or otherwise discovered the FMP's secret.

Branches

Now that you have joined, you will be assigned to a specific branch of the militia. Each branch is designated by the color of their uniforms or "Skirts".

Green Skirt: Army

Infantry soldier. Often deployed to the front lines. Trained in weapons, and often hand to claw combat as a means to fight back against the FMP ground forces. This branch is the largest of the forces, with most new recruits choosing to join these ranks as their "Starting point". Unfortunately, these newbies tend to wind up bound, leashed, & tied to a POW humiliation pole if they're not careful.

Blue Skirt: Air Force

For those with a penchant for flying. The Blue Skirts can be assigned a variety of roles crucial to AMAZON victories. Roles include troop transport, aerial support, and plane to drone combat. The good news is that FREMPs will ensure that your plane goes down without casualty... Unfortunately, the alternative they offer might be considered worse.

White Skirt: Navy

White Skirts are Seabound damsels facing off against aquatic FMP drones, and giving command support to ground forces. Many White Skirts tend to lean Strategist, using their smarts to take down enemies, rather than their brute strength. Unfortunately this often leaves them vulnerable if their ship is targeted by FREMP strike teams.

Black Skirt: Space Corps

The Black skirt forces were previously decimated by an FMP incursion. Nearly the entire Corp had been lured into a trap, leaving the AMAZON forces with very little in the way of Space warfare. Though some Black Skirts have been recovered through successful AMAZON raids, no one can quite understand how the FMP seemed to so easily take down an entire branch with ease. Some suspect Espionage by a Bellneck among their ranks.

Black Skirts exclusive to vetted, experienced players.

Yellow Skirt: Magical Corps

A very special branch of the AMAZON forces. Recruits among its ranks have shown innate magical ability ranging from simple manipulation of objects to full blown casting abilities. Those who join their ranks will be trained in the use of specialty weapons that draw from their Mana. While most FREMP forces refuse to acknowledge the existence of magic, there have been reports of a rogue Benefactor targeting Yellow Skirts for use as magical batteries. War Mages, as they are commonly called, are an incredibly high risk, high reward class & will be required to keep track of an additional token system. Magic comes at a cost, and the effects of a spell gone wrong can be incredibly detrimental.

Recommended for vetted, experienced players.

Bellneck: FMP Loyalist

Betrayers to the AMAZON cause, loyal to the benefactors. Though incredibly rare within the ranks, some bellnecks will slip past even the most tight security checks. Bellnecks must be wary of how much information they give away, as being outed as a Bellneck spy may mean incarceration or used in a way that will benefit the AMAZON forces.

Recommended for vetted, experienced players. Bellnecks will be playing in "Hard Mode".
